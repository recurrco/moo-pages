Questions HR usually asks

Do employees pay anything?

No. This is entirely employer-sponsored. Employees don't pay, order, or scan anything for what's in the fridge.

What if nobody eats one on a given day?

Your daily stock is set at 80% of headcount by default, specifically to keep this low. If you'd rather stock less and accept occasional stockouts, or more and accept occasional leftovers, we can adjust — talk to us.

Is this a subscription with a lock-in?

No flat monthly fee, no capacity you're forced to pay for. You're billed on what's actually stocked each month.

We work 6 days a week — can you still do this?

Yes. Pick Mon–Sat above and pricing adjusts for the extra stocking days.

What's actually in the fridge?

Three rotating flavours of fresh, sealed overnight oats (250g, 12–14g protein each) — restocked in your office every working morning before 9am.
